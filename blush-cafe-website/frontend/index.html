Our story

Started as a two-table pop-up, still feels like one.

Blush Café began in 2022 as a weekend pop-up run out of a friend's balcony — three coffee blends and a box of croissants. The response was loud enough that six months later, we had a real space off Saheed Nagar.

We kept the pop-up feeling on purpose: small batches, a short menu we actually believe in, and enough soft pink everywhere that you'll want a photo before your coffee gets cold.

  • Beans roasted locally, changed every season
  • Pastries baked fresh each morning, in-house
  • Free wifi, but we'll judge you kindly for using it
